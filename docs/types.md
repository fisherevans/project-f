# Types

In *Project F*, combat and creatures are shaped by five **types**. These are not a rock‑paper‑scissors weakness system, but instead **behavioral archetypes** that give structure to skills, statuses, and strategies. The **Xenolog** serves as the player’s compendium and progress tracker, tying these systems together.

---

## Creature Archetypes

Creatures in *Project F* embody one or more types. Their abilities, behaviors, and strategies align with the type’s identity.  

- **Kinetic**: Direct attackers, frequent use of block/interrupt.  
- **Thermal**: Status applicators, strong attrition damage, resist chip damage.  
- **Corrosive**: Slow but suffocating, damage that lingers and compounds.  
- **Voltaic**: High burst, fragile defenses, prone to self‑overextension.  
- **Growth**: Durable, sustaining fights, steady recovery and control.

### Kinetic
- **Theme**: Physical force, impact, blunt or piercing attacks.  
- **Combat Behavior**: Direct, relentless. Kinetic creatures often block, interrupt, and smash through defenses.  
- **Status Identity**: Weakened / Shattered (take more damage after heavy hits).  
- **Creature Archetypes**: Predators, armored beasts, melee bruisers.  
- **Strategy Feel**: Reduce damage taken while dishing out steady, punishing blows.  

### Thermal
- **Theme**: Heat, fire, freezing, phase changes.  
- **Combat Behavior**: Attrition through exposure — damage builds over time. Thermal creatures may burn or freeze foes while resisting similar effects.  
- **Status Identity**: Burning (exposure damage ramp), Frozen (delays ticks).  
- **Creature Archetypes**: Volcanic fauna, ice beasts, crystalline elementals.  
- **Strategy Feel**: Control through incremental harm; less vulnerable to chip damage.  

### Corrosive
- **Theme**: Acid, toxins, biological breakdown.  
- **Combat Behavior**: Suffocating, inevitable. Corrosive creatures spread poison and erosion that worsen the longer the fight lasts.  
- **Status Identity**: Poison (stack‑based decay), Decay (reduces healing/regen).  
- **Creature Archetypes**: Insects, fungi, sludge‑like organisms.  
- **Strategy Feel**: Outlast opponents by layering debilitating effects.  

### Voltaic
- **Theme**: Electricity, plasma, ionization.  
- **Combat Behavior**: Explosive bursts of damage at the cost of fragility. Voltaic creatures may stun or overload enemies but crumble under pressure.  
- **Status Identity**: Stunned (skip a tick), Overload (bonus damage from next hit).  
- **Creature Archetypes**: Electric serpents, storm flyers, plasma organisms.  
- **Strategy Feel**: Glass cannons — overwhelm quickly or fall apart.  

### Growth
- **Theme**: Vitality, regeneration, organic manipulation, muation.  
- **Combat Behavior**: Survival and sustain. Growth creatures heal, shield, or entangle foes, dragging fights into endurance contests.  
- **Status Identity**: Regeneration (heal per tick), Entangled (slowed or delayed skill input).  
- **Creature Archetypes**: Plant‑animal hybrids, symbiotes, resilient fauna.  
- **Strategy Feel**: Outlast and wear down through persistence.

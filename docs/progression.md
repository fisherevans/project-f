# Progression

The progression in *Project F* mixes **persistent upgrades** you carry between runs with **run‑specific boosts** that vanish when you extract (or if you fail). Your Animech grows by learning moves from creatures, improving its core stats, and carefully choosing loadouts before each expedition.

---

## High‑Level Loop

1. **HQ** — Prepare your **loadout** (4 moves), review upgrades, and launch to a planet.  
2. **Explore & Fight** — Defeat or capture creatures to earn **Experience (XP)** and **Research Points (RP)**.  
3. **Advance Forward** — Planets are one‑way, branching “**webs**” of zones. You can’t backtrack to earlier zones.  
4. **Extract** — Reach a terminal extraction point and spend **Elythium** (run‑only fuel) to return to HQ.  
5. **Bank Rewards** — Only what you successfully **extract** returns to HQ and becomes permanent.  
6. **Upgrade at HQ** — Spend XP and RP, adjust loadouts, and try a new route.

> **Elythium** is used **only** during a run as extraction fuel. It is **not** a persistent currency, cannot be stockpiled, and is never spent at HQ.

---

## Persistent vs Run‑Specific Progression

- **Persistent (Meta) Progression — at HQ**
  - **Animech XP Upgrades**: Shield, Sync, Shield Regeneration, Xenocrypt efficiency, and gated utility abilities (e.g., Dash) once discovered in the world.
  - **Move Unlocks via RP**: Creature‑specific Research Points unlock moves in that creature’s skill tree.
  - **Saved Loadouts**: Up to **10 named presets** of 4 moves each.

- **Run‑Specific (Temporary) Progression — during expeditions**
  - **Findable caches/mods** that grant temporary bonuses (e.g., stronger Poison, reduced Vulnerable penalty, bonus damage vs. Burning).  
  - These **expire** when you extract or are defeated and **do not** carry over to HQ.

> Lore note: Your **soul** pilots the Animech during runs. When you extract, your soul returns home and the temporary mods do not survive the transit; the Animech frame is effectively discarded.

---

## Research Points (RP)

**How you earn them**
- Earn **per creature type** when you **defeat** a creature; earn **more** when you **capture** it with the **Xenocrypt** (tradeoff detailed below).  
- RP gathered in a run must be **extracted** to be banked at HQ.

**How you spend them**
- Each creature has a small **skill tree** (typically **1–6 moves**) with prerequisites:
  - **Linear chains**: unlock in order.
  - **Starburst**: branches can be unlocked in any order.
  - **Branching/Converging**: some variety, light complexity.
- RP is **creature‑specific** — RP from creature *A* unlocks moves only in *A*’s tree.  
- After **fully unlocking** a creature’s tree, **future RP for that creature converts into sellable intel** for a to‑be‑defined economy/currency (details TBD).

**Outcome**
- Unlocked moves become **permanent** and can be slotted into loadouts at HQ.

## Animech Experience (XP)

**How you earn it**
- Gained from combat outcomes; **reduced** when using the Xenocrypt to capture (see below).  
- Like RP, XP must be **extracted** to be banked.

**How you spend it (at HQ)**
- **Core Stats** — escalating costs with soft caps:
  - **Shield**: e.g., +10 → +20 → +50 (costs rise at each tier).
  - **Sync**: similar stepping with increasing costs.
  - **Shield Regeneration**: incremental tiers with increasing costs.
- **Systems & Utilities (Gated)**:
  - Certain abilities (e.g., **Dash**) are **locked** until you discover related **modules** or reach story milestones out in the world.
  - Once discovered, you can invest XP to unlock/upgrade them.
- **Xenocrypt Efficiency**: Improve capture reliability or research yield over time.

> Design intent: XP paths are mostly **linear/laddered** with increasing price points, encouraging broad investment and long‑term planning rather than deep min‑max branching.

---

## Loadouts

- A **loadout** is **4 moves** chosen at HQ from your permanently unlocked move pool.  
- Loadouts **cannot** be changed mid‑run.  
- Save up to **10 named loadouts** for quick swapping before launch (copy/rename supported).

> Tip: Build specialized loadouts tailored to a planet’s threats or to a capture‑heavy strategy.

---

## Capture vs Defeat (Xenocrypt Tradeoff)

- **Defeat**: Normal XP and RP yields.  
- **Capture (Xenocrypt)**: **More RP** (faster move unlocks) but **less XP** for the Animech that fight.  
- The **Xenocrypt** is a dedicated combat action (see the Combat doc for timing and risks).  
- Upgrading the Xenocrypt at HQ can improve capture effectiveness and/or RP yield.

---

## Extraction & Return

- Planets are **one‑way, branching webs** of zones — routes may branch and later converge, but you **cannot backtrack** to previous zones.  
- **Multiple extraction nodes** exist as **terminal endpoints**; choose when to leave.  
- Extraction consumes **Elythium** (found during the run).  
- **Failure states**:
  - **Sync reaches 0**: the Animech is lost and **all unextracted XP/RP is lost**.
  - **Insufficient Elythium**: you cannot extract; continue forward to find more.
- On successful extraction, **bank** all carried XP/RP and return to HQ.

---

## Rewards Summary

- **Experience (XP)** → Spent at HQ for **Animech upgrades** (Shield, Sync, Regen, Xenocrypt efficiency, gated utilities like Dash).  
- **Research Points (RP)** → Spent at HQ to **unlock moves** in **creature‑specific** trees. Excess RP (after a tree is complete) converts to **sellable intel** (economy TBD).  
- **Elythium** → **Run‑only** fuel to **extract**. Never banked or spent at HQ.

## Completing a Run

Once you return to your ship, you will receive rewards for your exploration:

- **Expeirence Points**: Used to level up your Animech. This means:

  - Increasing it's base Shield and Integrity stats, or
  - Increasing the number of Animetric Cores it can hold at the same time

  > *Experience points can be uploaded digitally at Checkpoints.*

- **Research Points**: Are granted from captured specimen of combat experience. They can be used to unlock skills associated with that specimen.

  > *Research Points obtained during combat can be uploaded digitally at Checkpoints.*

- **Special Items**: Most cannot be fully processed by the Animech while on-planet and require equipment on your research vessel to decode or reconstruct. If successfully brought back to your ship, these items can be used to progress the story, unlock special Primortals, grant access to skills not available in skill trees, and other special benefits.

  > *Most Special Items must be physically returned to your ship.*

- **Memories**: Certain pieces of information found while in your Animech (such as a code on a note, or parts of a conversation) can be retained in your Soul's memory. Most memories will be used to progress the story, but some may also be used to unlock certain areas.

  > *Memories are retained the moment they happen. They do not need to be uploaded or physically returned to the ship.*

## Quick FAQ

**Q: Can I respec XP?**  
A: Not planned initially. The rising cost ladder encourages broad investment; a respec system may be considered later.

**Q: Can I change loadouts mid‑run?**  
A: No. Loadouts are locked once you launch, but you can prepare up to **10** named presets at HQ.

**Q: Do I keep XP/RP if I die?**  
A: No. You must **extract** with enough **Elythium** to bank your progress.

**Q: Is there gear or crafting?**  
A: Not in the core loop. Expect limited **items** and run‑mods; deeper crafting is out of scope for now.

## The XenoLog

The **XenoLog** is your compendium of knowledge, tracking progress across runs.  

- **Creature Entries**  
  - Species discovered, lore, and type alignment.  
  - Stats and core combat behaviors.  

- **Skill Trees**  
  - Each creature’s moves visualized in tree form.  
  - Progression tracked by invested Research Points.  

- **Skill Inspection**  
  - Tick‑by‑tick breakdown of how skills behave.  
  - Stance windows, status applications, damage values.  

- **Statuses Reference**  
  - Consolidated guide to all statuses and their effects.  

**Modes of Use**  

- **In the Field**: View‑only access while deployed.  
- **At HQ**: Plug the Xenolog into advanced systems to unlock new details, research progress, and skill trees.

## The Xenocrypt

The **Xenocrypt** is a tool that can be used to capture the essense of a creature, greatly increasing the **Research Points** received from combat. Once a creature is below a certain health threshold, the Xenocrypt can be used to capture them instead of defeating them.
